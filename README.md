Salesforce Training

Week 1 Work:

Day 1 - Salesforce CRM Basics
Day 1 covers Salesforce introduction, CRM, Salesforce Developer role, leads, contacts, accounts, opportunities, and basic CRM flow.

Day 2 - Salesforce Platform Basics
Day 2 covers Salesforce Platform, apps, objects, tabs, records, configuration, coding, and multi-tenant architecture.

Day 3 - Salesforce Data Modeling
Day 3 covers objects, fields, records, standard objects, custom objects, relationships, Schema Builder, formula fields, roll-up summary fields, and validation rules.

Day 4 - Salesforce Flow Builder and Automation
Day 4 covers Flow Builder, flow variables, screen flows, autolaunched flows, record-triggered flows, data elements, action elements, email alerts, Chatter posts, and global variables.
