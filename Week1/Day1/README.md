Day 1 - Salesforce CRM Basics

1. Salesforce:
Salesforce is a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management platform.
It helps organizations manage customers, sales, services, marketing, and business processes in one place.

2. CRM:
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.
It is a system used to manage customer details, communication history, sales activities, and customer support.

3. Need of Salesforce:
Salesforce is used to solve the problem of scattered customer data.
In many organizations, customer information may be stored in different Excel sheets, emails, and separate tools.
Salesforce brings all this information into one platform.

4. Uses of Salesforce:
Salesforce is used to:
- Store customer information
- Track leads and sales
- Manage customer communication
- Improve customer service
- Automate business processes
- Generate reports and dashboards

5. Salesforce Developer:
A Salesforce Developer builds and customizes Salesforce applications.
They use tools like Apex, APIs, automation, and Lightning Web Components to add extra features and business logic.

6. Basic CRM Flow:

Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer

CRM Flow Explanation:
Lead:
A lead is a person who may be interested in a product or service.
Contact:
A contact is a saved person detail in Salesforce.
Opportunity:
An opportunity is a possible deal, sale, or business process.
Customer:
A customer is a person who finally buys the product or service.

7. Real-Life Mapping: College Admission System
Account = College or Department
Contact = Student or Parent
Lead = Student enquiry
Opportunity = Admission process
Customer = Confirmed admitted student

8. Real-World Application:
A College Admission Management System can be built using Salesforce.
It can manage student enquiries, applications, admission status, fee details, and communication with students.
